Batter Up

A prospecting and accountability platform for Cheney Brothers sales teams.

Batter Up helps Cheney Brothers sales representatives, district sales managers, and leadership track prospective customer accounts from first contact through a first order. It uses a baseball metaphor for the sales pipeline: accounts advance through the bases, and a first order is a home run.

What it does

  • Keeps prospective account records — business details, contacts, estimated spend, and notes — in one place.
  • Tracks sales activities and pipeline stage as an account moves toward a first order.
  • Surfaces nearby accounts on a map so reps can plan in-person visits efficiently.
  • Computes performance metrics so reps and managers share the same picture of the pipeline.
  • Notifies managers when a rep raises a flag and needs help.

Who it's for

Batter Up is a private, internal business tool. Accounts are provisioned by Cheney Brothers for its employees — there is no public sign-up, and the app is not intended for anyone under 18.
