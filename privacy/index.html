1. Who we are and what this covers

Batter Up is an internal business tool used by sales teams to manage a pipeline of prospective customer accounts. In this policy, "we" and "us" refer to the operator of Batter Up, reachable at the address in section 11.

Accounts are provisioned by the organization that deploys the app for its employees — sales representatives, managers, and leadership. There is no public sign-up, and Batter Up is not a consumer product.

This policy covers the Batter Up mobile app for iOS and this website.

2. What data we collect

Account information

Your name, work email address, role (representative, manager, or VP), and an employee identifier. This information is provided by your employer when your account is created — you do not register for the app yourself.

Content you enter in the app

Records about prospective customer accounts, including business name, contact name and title, phone number, email address, address, estimated spend, and notes — along with the sales activities you log and the pipeline stage updates you make. This is business-to-business information about prospective customer accounts, entered by you as part of your work.

Location, only while the app is open

With your permission, the app uses your precise location while you are using it to show nearby customer accounts and, optionally, to attach a GPS pin when you log an in-person first visit. Location is never collected in the background, and is never sold or used for advertising or cross-app tracking. See section 4 for detail.

Device push token

An identifier for your device that lets us deliver notifications — for example, alerting a manager when a representative raises a flag for help.

Basic technical and log data

Information needed to operate and secure the service, such as records of requests to the backend and authentication events.

3. How we use it

We use the data to provide the app's core function:

  • Managing a pipeline of prospective customer accounts.
  • Computing performance metrics, such as a batting average and a 0–100 score.
  • Sending notifications related to your work in the app.
  • Letting managers and leadership see their team's activity, according to their role.
  • Operating, securing, and troubleshooting the service.

We do not use your data for advertising, and we do not sell it.

4. How location is used

Because location is sensitive, here is exactly how Batter Up handles it:

  • Location access is requested only as "while using the app."
  • It is triggered by something you do — opening the map, searching for nearby accounts, or logging a visit.
  • It is used to rank and display nearby customer accounts, and optionally to record where an in-person first visit took place.
  • It is not tracked in the background when the app is closed or not in use.
  • It is not sold, and not shared for advertising or cross-app tracking.

You can decline the location permission, or change it later in your device's Settings. The rest of the app continues to work; only the nearby-accounts and visit-pin features are affected.

5. Who we share it with

We share data only with the service providers that make the app work:

  • Supabase — hosts the database and handles authentication. Data is stored in the United States.
  • Expo — delivers push notifications to your device.
  • Apple — distributes the app.

We do not sell data. We do not share it with advertising networks or data brokers. Beyond the service providers listed above, we disclose data only if required by law.

6. Data security

Access to the app requires per-user authentication. The database enforces row-level security, so each employee can reach only the records their role permits: a representative sees only their own accounts, a manager sees their team, and the VP sees the organization. Data is encrypted in transit.

7. Data retention

Business records entered in the app are retained for as long as the account is active, or as long as the employer requires them for business purposes. Records may be removed at the employer's request.

8. Children

Batter Up is not directed to children and is not intended for anyone under 18. It is an employer-provided tool for business use.

9. Your rights

Batter Up is an employer-provided tool, and the records in it are business records belonging to your employer. If you are an employee and want to access, correct, or delete information, please contact your organization's administrator, or reach us at the address in section 11.

10. Changes to this policy

We may update this policy from time to time. When we do, we will change the "Last updated" date at the top of this page.
