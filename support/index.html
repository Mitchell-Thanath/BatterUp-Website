Frequently asked questions

How do I get an account?
Accounts are provisioned by your organization for its employees — there is no public sign-up. If you don't have access yet, contact your manager or your organization's administrator.
I can't sign in. What should I do?
Check that you're using your work email address. If it still doesn't work, contact your organization's administrator or email us at the address above.
Why does the app ask for my location?
Location is used only while the app is open — to show customer accounts near you and, optionally, to attach a GPS pin when you log an in-person first visit. It is never collected in the background, and never sold or used for advertising. You can decline the permission or change it later in your device's Settings; the rest of the app keeps working.
Why am I getting notifications?
Notifications cover activity relevant to your role — for example, a manager is alerted when a representative raises a flag for help. You can turn notifications off for Batter Up in your device's Settings.
Who can see the accounts I enter?
Access follows your role: a representative sees their own accounts, a manager sees their team's, and the VP sees the organization. See the Privacy Policy for details.
How do I correct or delete a record?
Records in Batter Up are business records belonging to your employer. Contact your organization's administrator, or email us at the address above.
